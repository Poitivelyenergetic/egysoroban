Mission & vision

Our message

Our mission is to provide an integrated program for developing children's mental abilities that goes beyond simply performing mental arithmetic operations, to become an effective means of discovering and developing a child's potential, and stimulating their mind daily in a way that positively impacts their academic level and life skills.

We work to build the child's confidence in himself and his mental abilities, and enhance his ability to think, concentrate and face challenges, while establishing the concept that the mind can be continuously developed through proper practice and training.

Our vision

At EGY Soroban Academy, we strive to build a generation with exceptional mental abilities by bringing the child to the highest levels of speed, accuracy, and focus in mental arithmetic, while achieving a balance between logical thinking and mental imagination at the same time.

We believe that the Japanese abacus is not just a means of learning arithmetic operations, but rather an integrated mental training similar to learning arts and professional skills.

How it works

From application to first level exam

01

Apply online

Send your child's details through the form below — takes about two minutes.

02

Assessment

Our team contacts you to arrange a placement assessment at the academy.

03

Placed in a level

Your child is placed at their KYU level based on age and starting point.

04

Weekly class & practice

One weekly 2-hour session, moving through 11 levels from Beginner to Expert.

05

Certified exam

An AIAMA-certified exam closes each level before moving to the next.
