Programs

Eleven levels, one path

Assessed once. 11 levels. A certified exam every step.

Ages 4–12 · 10th–8th KYU

Beginner

First contact with the abacus: counting, number sense, and single-digit addition and subtraction.

4–8 months per level1 session / week
Ages 4–12 · 7th–4th KYU

Intermediate

Multi-digit addition and subtraction, and the shift toward Anzan — calculating without touching the beads.

4–8 months per level1 session / week
Ages 4–12 · 3rd–1st KYU

Expert

Multiplication, division, and full mental (Anzan) calculation — the level competition-track students train at.

How it works

From application to first level exam

01

Apply online

Send your child's details through the form below — takes about two minutes.

02

Assessment

Our team contacts you to arrange a placement assessment at the academy.

03

Placed in a level

Your child is placed at their KYU level based on age and starting point.

04

Weekly class & practice

One weekly 2-hour session, moving through 11 levels from Beginner to Expert.

05

Certified exam

An AIAMA-certified exam closes each level before moving to the next.
