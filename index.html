About the academy

A calm, structured way into serious mental math

Egysoroban is an example soroban academy built to show what Egysoroban's real site could look like — swap this copy for your own. In this sample, Egysoroban teaches children to calculate on a real wooden soroban first, then to picture that same abacus in their mind — a technique called Anzan. The physical tool disappears; the skill stays.

Classes run in small groups of up to eight children, once a week, with short daily practice at home. Every student moves through eight structured levels at their own pace, from first counting beads to multiplying four-digit numbers entirely in their head.

Why soroban

What changes when a child trains this way

Soroban training is a physical, visual and mental exercise at once — that combination is what shows up in the classroom, not just on a math test.

Sharper focus

Tracking beads across ten columns trains sustained attention — children carry that focus into homework and exams.

Stronger working memory

Visualizing the abacus (Anzan) exercises the same memory muscles used for reading comprehension and multi-step problems.

Real calculation speed

By Level 5, most students add and subtract multi-digit numbers faster mentally than on a phone calculator.

Visible confidence

Levels and belt-style exams give children frequent, concrete proof that practice is paying off.

Programs

Eight levels, one path

Every student is placed by a short free assessment, then moves level by level — each one built on the last, like place value on a soroban itself.

Ages 4–6

Little Sparks

First contact with numbers and the soroban: counting, number sense, and comfort holding the tool.

~3 monthsIn-person
Ages 7–9

Foundation I–IV

Addition and subtraction mastery on the physical soroban, from single digits to large carries.

~12 monthsIn-person / online
Ages 9–12

Advanced V–VIII

Multiplication, division, and the shift to Anzan — calculating on a soroban that only exists in the mind.

~12 monthsIn-person / online
Ages 10+

Competition Track

Optional, by invitation once Level VI is complete — speed drills and entry to regional soroban competitions.

OngoingIn-person
