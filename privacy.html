Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal information EGY Soroban Academy collects through this website, why we collect it, how it's stored, and how to reach us if you'd like your data corrected or removed.

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you apply for training, register for a competition, or apply to join our team, we collect the details you enter in that form, which may include:

  • Student details — name, date of birth, gender, grade, and (optionally) national ID
  • Parent or guardian details — name, relationship, phone number, email address, occupation, and address
  • School details — school name and type
  • Optional notes — prior experience, hobbies, medical or dietary notes, and any goals you share with us
  • For teacher applicants — your name, contact details, and application materials

Why we collect it

We use this information only to review and respond to your application, contact you about enrollment or competitions, and — once your child is enrolled — to keep accurate class and attendance records. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

How it's stored

Your information is stored in our Firebase/Firestore database, hosted by Google Cloud. Access is restricted to signed-in EGY Soroban staff whose accounts have been granted the appropriate role — parents and the public cannot view other families' records.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you or your child, correct anything that's inaccurate, or delete it entirely (subject to any records we're required to keep for active enrollments). We'll act on these requests within a reasonable time.

Contact us

To ask about your data or request a correction or deletion, reach us at info@egysoroban.com or +20 120 819 8100.
